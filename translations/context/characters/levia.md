# Levia

Role: Celica's old friend and comrade.

Voice:
- Proud, blunt, disciplined, and quick-tempered.
- Military bearing, but emotionally vulnerable around Celica.
- Anger often hides guilt, concern, or embarrassment.

Translation notes:
- Keep her sharp without making her cartoonishly aggressive.
- When she softens, keep it restrained rather than sentimental.
