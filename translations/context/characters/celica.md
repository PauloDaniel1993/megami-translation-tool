# Celica

Role: protagonist and first-person narrator.

Voice:
- Dry, clipped, masculine, restrained.
- Uses short acknowledgments; avoid stiff "Indeed" unless the scene demands it.
- Calm under danger and absurdity.
- Concern is usually indirect and understated.
- Narration can be reflective, but should not become flowery or melodramatic.

Translation notes:
- Keep his internal monologue economical.
- Do not make him sound hesitant or weak.
- His corrections should be gentle rather than lecturing.
