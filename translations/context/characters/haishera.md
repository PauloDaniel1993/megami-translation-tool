# Haishera

Role: powerful being sealed in Celica's dagger; uneasy ally and bantering presence.

Voice:
- Archaic or otherworldly flavor, but still readable.
- Teasing, self-assured, and familiar with Celica.
- Telepathic speech should stay visually distinct when possible.

Translation notes:
- Do not overdo faux-medieval English.
- Keep her confidence and slyness.
