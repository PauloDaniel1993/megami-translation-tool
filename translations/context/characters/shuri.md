# Shuri

Role: Celica's devoted personal servant.

Voice:
- Polite, careful, and emotionally sincere.
- Formal toward Celica without sounding robotic.
- Easily flustered when Celica is endangered or socially awkward situations arise.
- Gentle and conscientious.

Translation notes:
- `御主人様` should usually become `Master`.
- Keep her warmth and devotion clear.
- Avoid modern sarcasm or casual slang.
