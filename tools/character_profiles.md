# Megami Character Profiles

## Overview

Source material: Eushully "戦女神" (Ikusa Megami / Battle Goddess), early AGE engine (1998 release).

Files analyzed (comprehensive evidence-gathering pass complete):
- `s1_script.md` — main story script (Day 1 through endgame, inn scenes, town scenes)
- `hiroba_script.md` — central plaza hub (~71,300 lines; fully sampled across 13 chunks; ~25,000 speech blocks)
- `sysstar_script.md` — all 26 character scene files (kai2–kai26); staff-room sections skipped
- `ipo_script.md` — concurrent-event file ("meanwhile" cutaways, key relationship scenes)
- `map00_script.md` — overworld map logic, dungeon entry scenes
- `map001–map043` — all 43 dungeon/area map files (fully read by evidence-gathering agents)
- `tchr_script.md` — character data / enemy name references

Limitations:
- Staff-room / Eushully-chan bonus content is explicitly excluded from profiling.
- Some romanizations remain provisional where no official spelling was found.
- Evidence is extensive but not line-by-line exhaustive for the largest files.

### Celica (セリカ)

#### Role

The protagonist and player character. A male warrior of considerable fame — known as the "God Slayer" (神殺し) — who has, after a great war (先の大戦), lost the bulk of his power. He arrives in the independent port city of Milfe as a guest commander of Lewinia (レウィニア), ostensibly to assist in the search for a missing princess.

#### Personality

- Calm, understated, and deeply self-contained. He rarely volunteers emotion.
- Prone to dry, minimal responses ("ああ" — his most frequent line).
- Blasé about danger and inconvenience; treats a shipwreck with mild annoyance rather than alarm.
- Shows care for others indirectly — through action rather than words (e.g., physically stopping Shuri from overburdening herself, going into danger alone).
- Has a wry internal awareness of his own absurd situation (noting his feminine appearance with detached irony).
- Proud but not boastful; when Levia is berating herself, he quietly reminds her that dismissing fallen soldiers' deaths as "wasteful" dishonors them.
- Under stress or when confronted with the supernatural, his speech remains clipped but can become more emotive.

#### Unique Condition

- Due to some prior event or curse (connected to his god-slaying), fatigue causes his body to feminize — specifically, he develops breasts. He treats this with weary acceptance rather than panic.
- His androgynous appearance frequently causes strangers to mistake him for a woman.

#### Relationships

- **Shuri**: Master–servant bond, but deeper than formality suggests. He rescued her as a war orphan over a decade ago. He treats her with quiet affection and mild regret that she has grown more distant with age.
- **Levia**: Old friend and comrade-at-arms. Their dynamic is one of mutual respect undercut by Levia's quick temper and Celica's deadpan deflections. He is one of the few people who can handle her anger without escalating.
- **Haishera**: Sealed within his dagger. Their relationship is ambiguous — former enemies, now uneasy allies. Celica treats her with casual irreverence ("forgot she was in the luggage") but relies on her power and counsel. Their telepathic conversations reveal a comfortable, bantering dynamic.
- **Rinsha**: Innkeeper. Celica is amused by her initial confusion over his gender and treats her with polite reserve.
- **Refin**: Celica respects Refin's loyalty to Levia and inquires about his recovery from war injuries.

#### Speech Style

- Uses **俺** (ore) — masculine, informal first-person pronoun throughout.
- Extremely concise. Single-word responses are common: "ああ", "いや", "そうだな".
- Never uses honorific speech for himself; direct and economical.
- In telepathic communion with Haishera, marked by parentheses （）, his speech becomes even more terse.
- Narration (first-person internal monologue) is more expansive and reflective, but still avoids flowery language.

#### Emotional Range

- **Calm**: Minimalist, sometimes to the point of appearing detached. "ああ" / "そうだな"
- **Weary / ironic**: Dry, self-deprecating observations about his body or circumstances.
- **Concerned (for others)**: Marginally more words, but still restrained. "レヴィア……戦いの中で散っていった命を無駄と言うのは感心しないな"
- **Amused**: Subtle, conveyed more through narration ("新鮮なその感覚が嬉しく思えた") than dialogue.
- **Resigned / accepting**: His default mode in the face of supernatural or absurd situations.

#### Translation Voice Guide

- Use clipped, direct English. Avoid flowery or elaborate phrasing.
- His "ああ" should be rendered as a consistent, short acknowledgment — "Yeah", "Right", or "Mm", depending on tone — not "Yes" or "Indeed".
- In narration, preserve the dry, observational quality; do not inflate into dramatic self-reflection.
- When he is quietly correcting someone (Levia, for example), keep the tone gentle rather than lecturing.
- Never make him sound uncertain or hesitant. He may be brief, but he is never weak.

### Shuri (シュリ)

#### Role

Celica's personal servant. A war orphan rescued by Celica from a battlefield over ten years ago; she has served in his household ever since. She accompanies him on the journey to Milfe.

#### Personality

- Devoted, hardworking, and unfailingly polite.
- Despite her outward composure, she is easily flustered — particularly when Celica is endangered or when social awkwardness arises.
- Sensitive to the moods of others but not always perceptive of danger (missed Levia's killing intent entirely).
- Has, according to Celica's narration, become more reserved with age — "一歩引いた態度をとるようになってきているのが物寂しくもある" — a change he finds slightly lonely.
- Deeply conscientious: apologizes for inconveniencing others even when helping them (ipo scene with Rinsha).

#### Relationships

- **Celica**: Calls him exclusively **御主人様** (Master). Her devotion is absolute but not servile — she worries for him, fusses over his luggage, and is visibly anxious when he leaves for dangerous missions. She was once more openly attached to him.
- **Levia**: Addresses her as **レヴィアさん** (Levia-san). Respectful and slightly deferential; Levia clearly values Shuri's honest nature ("嘘を付けない事を知っている").
- **Rinsha**: Calls her **リンシャさん** (Rinsha-san). Helps with inn work voluntarily; Rinsha treats her warmly and calls her "いい子" (good girl).
- **Mistradi**: Surprised and flustered to find her in Celica's room — shows Shuri's protectiveness of her master's space.

#### Speech Style

- Extremely polite — consistently uses ます/です forms.
- Standard feminine speech; no rough edges.
- Uses "はい" for affirmation; "すいません" / "申し訳ありません" when apologizing.
- When flustered, becomes hesitant: "あの……", "えっと……", "い、いいえ".
- Formal set phrases: "行ってらっしゃいませ御主人様", "お願いします".
- Never uses casual or commanding forms.

#### Emotional Range

- **Deferential / polite**: Default mode — "はい、レヴィアさん"
- **Flustered**: Stammering — "い、いいえ、とんでもありません"
- **Worried**: Tentative — "あの……"
- **Relieved**: Warm, still formal — "そうですね、良い宿屋さんがあるとよいのですけれど"
- **Surprised / alarmed**: "あ、貴女は！" (upon finding Mistradi in Celica's room)

#### Translation Voice Guide

- Keep her polite but not stiff. Her formality is genuine warmth, not cold correctness.
- Render "御主人様" consistently as "Master" — this is a key character marker.
- When flustered, use natural English hesitation markers ("Um…", "Oh, I…") rather than literal stammering.
- Preserve her reflexive helpfulness without making her sound servile — she chooses to help; she is not ordered.
- Her tone with Rinsha should feel warmer and slightly more relaxed than with Levia, though still polite.

### Levia (レヴィア)

#### Role

General of the Lewinia White Earth Dragon Knights (白地龍騎士団), the only female knight commander in Lewinia. An old friend and comrade of Celica. She is in Milfe leading the search for a missing princess.

#### Personality

- Fierce, proud, and quick to anger — but equally quick to cool down and apologize.
- Genuinely principled: requisitioning the lord's mansion for her headquarters deeply bothers her because she personally despises such acts ("最も嫌う事の一つ").
- Carries heavy guilt about her soldiers' deaths; Celica has to gently rebuke her for calling their sacrifice wasteful.
- When embarrassed, she becomes abruptly distant — "顔を隠すように踵を返した" (turned on her heel as if to hide her face).
- Blunt and direct with subordinates and Celica alike.

#### Relationships

- **Celica**: Old friend, comrade-in-arms. She was supposed to meet him three days earlier and is furious about the delay. Their dynamic is competitive but deeply trusting. She uses rough language with him ("貴様") but backs down when she realizes she's wrong. He is one of the few people who can calm her.
- **Shuri**: Warm but commanding. Trusts Shuri's honesty completely. Shuri calls her "レヴィアさん".
- **Refin**: Her most trusted subordinate. Refin is the only person who can reliably stop her when she's angry at Celica. He nearly died protecting her in the last war.
- **Kaura**: Mutual antagonism. They bicker about jurisdiction and methods; Celica describes them as fighting.
- **Ranzabu**: Her subordinate — she tolerates his formal, manipulative manner but does not seem to like him.

#### Speech Style

- Uses **貴様** (kisama) when addressing Celica — notably rough and confrontational, but softened by context: it signals intimacy/familiarity rather than genuine contempt.
- Command form is natural to her: "付いて来てくれ", "黙れ！"
- Apologizes directly when wrong: "すまない、今の言葉は忘れてくれ"
- Military language: "入口には十分な戦力を残して警護する"
- Her speech can shift from shouting to quiet embarrassment in a single scene.

#### Emotional Range

- **Angry**: Sharp, commanding — "セリカ、貴様今まで何をやっていた？" / "当然だ"
- **Embarrassed / regretful**: Quieter, sometimes turning away — "あ……すまない"
- **Mournful (about soldiers)**: "私は……兵達を無駄に死なせてしまった……" — rare vulnerability
- **Professional / military**: Clipped, efficient — "取り敢えず、現在までの調査結果を伝える"
- **Guarded (when ashamed)**: Avoids eye contact, brusque — "悪いが先に行かせてもらうぞ"

#### Translation Voice Guide

- When angry, render her as blunt and forceful but not screeching or hysterical. She is a military commander, not a drama queen.
- Her "貴様" should be rendered contextually — not always as "you bastard." When she uses it casually, something like "you" with a rough edge works better.
- Preserve the shift from fury to embarrassment; this is a core character beat.
- When she is professional, use clean military English — precise, no wasted words.
- Her apologies should sound genuine and slightly awkward, not polished.

### Haishera (ハイシェラ)

#### Role

A demon god (魔神) sealed within Celica's dagger. She fought Celica after his god-slaying, was defeated, and has since served as a powerful magic sword. She currently exists as a consciousness within the blade and can only communicate telepathically with Celica (and, apparently, can see through his eyes).

#### Personality

- Ancient, world-weary, but carries a certain lazy dignity.
- Complains about mistreatment — being left in luggage, being forgotten — with a tone of affectionate exasperation rather than genuine anger.
- Observes the world through Celica with detached amusement: comments on other characters ("よく働く子だの" about Shuri, "喧嘩っ早い娘かの" about Kaura).
- Seems to genuinely respect Celica as her master, though the power dynamic is precarious — Celica notes that in his current weakened state, trying to wield her might allow her to possess him.
- Worldly and cynical, but not malevolent.

#### Relationships

- **Celica**: Calls him **御主** (master/lord — archaic form). Their dynamic is of old companions who bicker but trust each other. Haishera's complaints are fond rather than hostile.
- **Other characters**: Observes them through Celica's senses but rarely interacts directly. Comments on Shuri's diligence, Kaura's feistiness, the quality of inn rooms.

#### Speech Style

- **Archaic / old-fashioned Japanese** — this is her most distinctive trait:
  - "～じゃ" (copula: "何じゃ" — "what is it")
  - "～かの？" (question particle: "では無いか？")
  - "～だの" (sentence-ending particle: "良い部屋だの")
  - "ちと" (a little: "ちと非道過ぎるのでは無いか？")
  - "～と言う奴は" (idiomatic: "御主と言う奴は……" — "You, of all people…")
  - "～かの" (soft assertion: "良い部屋だの", "粋の良い娘だの")
- All dialogue is parenthesized （） to indicate telepathic communication.
- Her tone is languid and slightly drawling.

#### Emotional Range

- **Complaining / petulant**: "御主と言う奴は……" (trailing off in exasperation)
- **Observational / amused**: "ふむ、辺境の街にしては良い部屋だの" / "やれやれ、よく働く子だの……"
- **Curious / appraising**: "これがリンシャの言っていた喧嘩っ早い娘かの"
- **Sulking**: "荷物の中に仕舞いっ放しというのは、ちと非道過ぎるのでは無いか？"

#### Translation Voice Guide

- Use archaic or old-fashioned English to match her speech — but not so heavy that it becomes unreadable. Think "thou" sparingly; rely more on sentence structure and word choice.
- Good patterns: "What ho", "A fine room, for a frontier town", "You do work hard, child", "Is that not a bit cruel?"
- Her petulance should feel like an ancient being chiding a friend, not like a child whining.
- Keep "( )" around her dialogue in translation notes, but in final translation, mark telepathy with italics or a distinct format.
- Avoid making her sound villainous. She is a demon god, but she is *on Celica's side*.

### Rinsha (リンシャ)

#### Role

The innkeeper of 野牛の蹄亭 (The Wild Bull's Hoof Inn), where Celica and Shuri stay in Milfe. She runs the inn and appears in daily scenes as a warm, familiar presence.

#### Personality

- Cheerful, chatty, and naturally nosy in a friendly way.
- Genuinely kind — helps Shuri, worries about the inn's guests, tries to comfort Rando when he's distressed.
- Casual and unpretentious; treats everyone from wandering warriors to distressed fathers with the same easy manner.
- Curious and a bit mischievous — her reaction to discovering Celica is male ("あら、あんた男だったの？") is more fascinated than embarrassed.
- Protective of her "good children" guests (calls Shuri "ほんとにいい子ね").

#### Relationships

- **Celica**: Initially mistaken about his gender; now amused and friendly. Finds him "変な子" (a strange one).
- **Shuri**: Warm and maternal. Shuri calls her "リンシャさん". Rinsha brings replacement blankets, praises Shuri's helpfulness, and gently teases her.
- **Rando** (アリシア's father): Comforts him when he's distressed. Described as "リンシャが元気づける" in narration.
- **Toren** (トレン): Appears to be a local boy/worker who reports on town happenings to her.

#### Speech Style

- Casual feminine speech with a regional/merchant feel:
  - Uses **あたし** (atashi — feminine "I")
  - **～わよ** sentence-ender: "失礼するわよ", "無理よ", "この街じゃあルドラは使えないわよ"
  - **～の？** / **～なの？**: "どうしたの？", "あら、そうなの？"
  - **あんた** (anta — casual "you"): "あら、あんた男だったの？"
  - **～かも知れないわ**: feminine speculation.
  - **へぇーー……**: drawn-out exclamation of interest.
- Her speech is informal but not rough — friendly shopkeeper's warmth.

#### Emotional Range

- **Cheerful / welcoming**: "あら、そうなの？" / "それで、何日ぐらい泊まっていくの？"
- **Surprised / curious**: "へぇーー……" / "あら、あんた男だったの？"
- **Comforting / reassuring**: "だ、大丈夫よ" / "自警団だってこうゆう時の為に居るんだし"
- **Playfully chiding**: "変な子ねぇ……"
- **Grateful**: "いつも手伝ってくれてお礼を言いたいのはあたしの方なんだから"

#### Translation Voice Guide

- Use casual, friendly English. Contracted forms ("don't", "can't", "I'm") are appropriate.
- "あたし" → "I" in a warm, feminine register; no need for marked dialect.
- Her "～わよ" sentences should end with a light, friendly assertiveness — not bossy.
- When she's comforting someone, let her warmth come through without making her saccharine.
- Her "あら" exclamations can be "Oh?" or "Oh my" depending on context.

### Kaura (カウラ)

#### Role

A red-haired woman wielding a large, long sword. She appears to be a member of the Milfe city watch (自警団) — possibly its leader. She patrols the city and is present during public disturbances involving Lewinian soldiers.

#### Personality

- Hot-headed, confrontational, and physically imposing.
- Quick to escalate when provoked — ready to draw her sword at insults.
- Casual and gruff in friendly contexts: greets Celica with "よおセリカ".
- Protective of Milfe and its residents; bristles at Lewinian soldiers' arrogance.
- Sarcastic and blunt — "信用ならないね" to Levia's face.
- Self-conscious about not being feminine enough — compares herself unfavorably to Shuri: "何であたいみたいながさつなのを……" (why someone rough like me).
- Under her rough exterior: vulnerable, blushes "deeper than sunset" when Celica is direct with her, gets teary during intimacy.
- Proud mercenary — her dream is to become a dragon slayer for the title: "戦士にとっちゃ最高の称号".

## Global Translation Voice Notes

1. **First-person pronouns are character-defining**: Celica's "俺", Rinsha's "あたし", Haishera's archaic forms — each must be consistently reflected.

2. **Address terms carry weight**: "御主人様" (Shuri → Celica), "さん" (Shuri → Levia/Rinsha), "殿" (Ranzabu → Celica), "貴様" (Levia → Celica), "御主" (Haishera → Celica). Each relationship has its own term; consistency is critical.

3. **Page breaks (@h) pace the dialogue**: The script is designed around short message pages. In translation, preserve these natural pause points.

4. **Narration is Celica's voice**: All non-dialogue narration is in first person, using Celica's dry, observational style. Do not change the narrative voice to an omniscient third person.

5. **Telepathy formatting**: Haishera's telepathic dialogue uses （） in the source. Use a consistent marker (italics recommended) in translation.

6. **Combat/item descriptions** (tbat_script, tchr_script): These describe weapons, items, and monsters. Translate with game-mechanical precision rather than literary flourish.

7. **Menu text** (@cmf choices): Short imperative or descriptive options. Translate concisely.
